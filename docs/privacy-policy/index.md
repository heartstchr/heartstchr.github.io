# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 2025**

## Introduction

At Stack Seekers ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at [stackseekers.com](https://stackseekers.com) or use our web development services.

This policy applies to all information collected through our website, services, and any related applications, whether online or offline. By using our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

- **Contact us** for inquiries or consultations via email
- **Request project quotes** through our contact form
- **Submit project requests** through our project request form

This information includes:

- **Name** (full name)
- **Email address**
- **Project details** and requirements
- **Service preferences** (selected from our service options)
- **Budget range** (if provided)
- **Any other information** you choose to provide in your communications

### Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain technical information through Google Analytics:

- **Device Information:** IP address, device type, operating system, browser type and version
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, referral sources
- **Location Data:** General geographic location based on IP address
- **Analytics Data:** Website performance metrics and user behavior patterns
- **Traffic Classification:** Internal vs external traffic (via cookies)

This information helps us understand how visitors use our website and improve our services.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:

### Service Delivery

- **Project Management:** Planning, executing, and delivering web development services
- **Communication:** Responding to inquiries, providing project updates, and technical support
- **Quality Assurance:** Ensuring project requirements are met and maintaining service standards

### Business Operations

- **Client Management:** Maintaining client accounts and service history
- **Legal Compliance:** Meeting regulatory requirements and legal obligations

### Website Improvement

- **Analytics:** Understanding user behavior to improve website functionality and user experience
- **Performance Monitoring:** Identifying and resolving technical issues
- **Content Optimization:** Enhancing content based on user engagement patterns

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:

### Technical Safeguards

- **Encryption:** All data transmission uses industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption
- **Secure Storage:** Personal data is stored securely with restricted access
- **Access Controls:** Limited access to personal information
- **Regular Updates:** Security patches and system updates applied promptly

### Organizational Measures

- **Data Minimization:** We collect only the information necessary for our services
- **Confidentiality Agreements:** All team members maintain strict confidentiality
- **Incident Response:** Established procedures for handling potential security breaches

**Important Note:** While we implement security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your information to the best of our ability.
