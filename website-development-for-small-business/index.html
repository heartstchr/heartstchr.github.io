Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a small business website cost?

Pricing depends on scope. A professional 5-page website starts with a fixed-scope quote after our initial call. No hidden fees, no hourly surprises.

How long does it take to build?

Most small business websites are completed in 2-4 weeks. More complex sites with custom features may take 4-6 weeks.

Will my website show up on Google?

Yes. Every site includes SEO fundamentals: meta tags, schema markup, sitemap, fast loading, and mobile optimization. Local SEO is included for small businesses.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. I can integrate a CMS like Notion, Sanity, or a custom admin panel so you can update content without touching code. Maintenance plans are also available.
