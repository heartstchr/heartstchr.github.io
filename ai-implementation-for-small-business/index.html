Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need technical staff or a data team?

No. I handle the technical work end to end and explain every step in plain language. Your team just needs to know their own workflows.

How much does AI implementation cost?

You get a fixed-scope quote after the readiness assessment, before any build begins. Small engagements start with one quick win so you see a return before scaling up.

Is my business data safe to use with AI?

That is exactly what the readiness assessment answers. I show where personal data must be redacted, which tasks can use cloud models, and where a self-hosted model is the safer choice.

We already tried AI tools and got nowhere. Can you help?

Often yes. I start by looking at what you already pay for, keep what earns its place, and connect or replace the rest so it fits how your business actually runs.
